# A half-turn of charge invisible to *all* locally learned prime-field rows

**Result.** Change the total charge of a flow over the cyclic group Z/4 from 0 to 2. On the Boolean encoding below, fix any local scope width w and take a sufficiently large three-edge-connected graph. A **single Boolean word** falsifies the resulting CNF but satisfies every affine equation that follows from raw clauses on any scope of at most w bits, over *every field simultaneously*. Thus combining and exchanging consequences of those linear summaries alone cannot refute the CNF. The word is an actual model of its balanced twin. It is not an argument against algorithms reading the raw clauses by other means. The [native solver](../src/pnp_lab/cyclic_four.py) finds and checks a ring-valued contradiction on this family, including after independent permutations of the four state names at each edge and Boolean sign/ID scrambling.

## Raw encoding, including the helper semantics

On each oriented edge put a state t in Z/4. Choose an independent permutation of the state names in the four Boolean slots x₀,x₁,x₂,x₃. Add the six binary exclusions and, using one fresh Boolean h, the two clauses (x₀ OR x₁ OR h), (NOT h OR x₂ OR x₃). Exactly one slot is true. If a first-pair slot is true, h=false; if a second-pair slot is true, h=true. Thus h is a unary function of the state, even when the two chosen slots do not form a parity class. It is not an unaccounted free variable in the argument below.

For each cubic vertex add 48 ternary clauses: forbid every triple of states whose oriented divergence is not the given charge using one negative slot literal per edge. The other 16 triples are allowed. Total size is 5|E| bits and 8|E|+48|V| clauses, all of width at most three. The graph and permutations are part of the generator only. The tested solver sees one final raw CNF, without the state map, paired instance, native charges or reference witness. This is a flow over Z/4, **not** over the four-element field (which has characteristic 2).

On a connected graph a charge vector is realizable iff its total is zero modulo four. Necessity follows by summing all vertices. Sufficiency follows by assigning flows along a spanning tree towards a root; subtraction, but no division, is needed. The [generator](../src/pnp_lab/four_flow.py) takes a nonzero planted flow and computes its charges, then increases one charge by two for the UNSAT twin. The reference word, including every private h, is the planted assignment for the original balanced charges. The altered negative triple at the changed vertex rules it out in the UNSAT twin.

## Why all fields can agree on a false candidate

The proof has three steps. Write S for any local Boolean scope which misses **all bits of at least one edge**; w<|E| suffices because every bit, including every helper, belongs to exactly one edge. Pool as many raw clauses supported within S as desired.

1. **Repair one unobserved charge.** At an endpoint of the omitted edge each vertex clause necessarily mentions a slot of that edge. No such vertex clause is in the pool. Subtract two from the charge at this endpoint. The repaired graph has total charge zero, and every model of its flow system satisfies the selected pool, independent of how S was found. A locally entailed linear equation must hold on the entire repaired flow coset.
2. **Two independent cycle perturbations isolate one edge.** On a legal word the linear form has the shape ∑ₑ fₑ(tₑ): every slot and every helper is a unary function of one state. Coefficients zero outside S can be included. For a given oriented e take two cycles whose edge sets meet only at e, and orient their unit circulations C,D with Cₑ=Dₑ=1. Such cycles exist whenever two edge-disjoint alternative paths connect the endpoints after removing e (in particular for a three-edge-connected graph). For the tested prisms there is a direct proof: for a ring edge use the rest of that ring for one path and the opposite ring edge plus its two rungs for the other; for a rung use the square to its left and the square to its right. For any repaired flow t the four flows t, t+aC, t+bD, t+aC+bD are also repaired flows. Add their constant linear equations with signs +, −, −, +. Terms on edges other than e cancel, leaving

       fₑ(x+a+b) − fₑ(x+a) − fₑ(x+b) + fₑ(x) = 0

   for all a,b,x in Z/4; all x occur since one may first translate along C. Taking x=0 gives fₑ(x)=constant+φₑ(x), with φₑ a homomorphism from Z/4 into the additive group of the coefficient field. This holds for every edge. Since 4φₑ(1)=0, φₑ=0 in characteristic other than 2. In characteristic 2, 2φₑ(1)=0, so only the parity of x can matter. This proof does **not** average over a power of two in characteristic two. It works for arbitrary fields, not only the prime fields exercised in the experiment.
3. **One common word works for every pool and field.** Use the balanced twin's original planted edge word t*. Its edge parities satisfy the parity demands of *every* repaired graph: adding/subtracting two never changed a demand mod 2. In each repaired graph, lift these same parities to a genuine Z/4 flow. To see this, start with the edge parities as integer 0/1 representatives. Subtract their divergences from the repaired charges. The differences are even in Z/4 and have zero sum there, so halving gives a zero-total demand in Z/2. Solve the binary demand by a spanning tree, double its flow and add it to the initial representatives. The resulting Z/4 flow has exactly the prescribed parities. Each fₑ in step 2 has the same value on that lift as on t*. Thus the row holds on the reference word. While the charge-repaired graph used to prove the statement may vary with S, the reference word **does not**. Independent bit sign flips and coordinate permutations simply transform both equations and the common word affinely.

In particular, setting w=15 or 25 and taking enough edges gives an obstruction even if complete entire cubic stars or adjacent pairs of cubic stars are inspected, not merely the smaller regions chosen by the present general-purpose field learner. Allowing arbitrary subsequent sound logical inferences *from the collected equations alone*, even across fields with Boolean domains enforced, still leaves that common model. A solver that goes back to nonlinear input clauses, uses fresh variables encoding a split into low/high bits, or works over rings with additive order four need not obey this barrier.

## Finite checks and cost accounting

The [experiment](../experiments/four_flow.py) independently enumerates the 16 legal four-state assignments of a single 15-Boolean-bit star and the 64 compatible state models in the 25-bit union of two adjacent stars. These legal-state enumerations are exact for those complete local tables because the block helpers have unique extensions. Over F2 the affine dimensions are respectively 8 and 13; over F3,F5,F7 they are 9 and 15. In every case adding the reference word leaves the rank unchanged despite the raw violated star. No inference about infinitely many fields is based on these finite ranks.

On the largest 64-vertex example (480 Boolean variables, 3,840 clauses), native discovery verifies SAT on the balanced case and produces a checked 64-term contradiction with residual right side 2 on the unbalanced one. Across all tested sizes the reference word directly satisfied all 708 explored raw local F2/F3 rows on bad inputs. The [tests](../tests/test_four_flow.py) independently enumerate every Boolean word for two small independently labelled domains and every charge pair, check lifting parity words on K₄ and verify both scrambled twins. The [results](../results/four_flow.json) distinguish finite checks from the all-size theorem. Neither field-blindness nor the native success constitutes a bound for unrestricted SAT.
