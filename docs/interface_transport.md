# How propagation collapses the previous exponential transport layer

On the [phase-12 direct-transport antichain](transport_obstruction.md) there are 2^d raw residuals after d designated tag splits. The [private-helper variant](transport_padding.md) also defeated the earlier affine labels, blocked-clause removal and anchored comparisons in that order. This time we keep the raw residuals but change how a witness map is justified. **Every ordered pair at the same tag depth admits a proof by unit propagation with zero further decisions**, for both constructions. Hence the implemented streaming pairwise deletion retains one representative per tag layer. The theorem ends when tag splitting ends; unbounded branching on unrelated variables is not made polynomial by this statement.

## Interface map independent of vertex or tag names

From the target B alone, build the implication graph of its binary clauses: (l∨r) gives ¬l→r and ¬r→l. Two signed literals in a strongly connected component have equal truth in every target model. This use of binary implication graphs is standard; see [Aspvall, Plass and Tarjan](https://doi.org/10.1016/0020-0190(79)90002-4). The map proposal declines contradictory components rather than assigning a truth value to them.

Call a target clause *external* if it is not already tautological after identifying equivalent signed literals. Mark all variables in external clauses. In each pair of complementary components choose a named variable, preferring a marked member whenever present. Send every target variable to that representative with the appropriate sign in the source. No graph isomorphism search is performed; when coordinates have no meaningful correspondence this proposal can fail. Its correctness never relies on trusting the graph algorithm: the [checker](derived_transport.md) verifies each pulled target clause against the raw source, with original clause IDs.

**Sufficient criterion.** Suppose each external raw clause of B is in A and, whenever marked literals agree up to sign in B's binary components, their corresponding same-named literals also agree up to that sign via paths in A's binary implication graph. Then the proposed substitution is RUP for every target clause. An internal clause maps to a tautology. For an external clause, falsifying its image falsifies each representative. Following binary implications in A propagates those values to all literals of the original clause, which is itself in A. Unit propagation must reach a conflict. This only requires correspondence between interface literals, not an isomorphism of all inner variables. Parallel cases outside this criterion are harmless: the final replay accepts or rejects the actual proof.

## Why the criterion applies at every tag depth

In each completed switch module of the phase-12 graph, degree-two parity constraints and the selected wire equalities form binary implication paths. Re-routing five internal degree-two vertices changes the *lengths* of two paths, not which named cubic endpoint ports they connect. The internal path variables do not occur in external clauses. Unassigned modules are unchanged on both sides, including their guarded clauses. Distinct modules have their own interior ports. Hence on the external variables the signed equality relation read by the binary paths is the same for every word at a fixed depth. New clauses that vary between the chosen words are wire equalities and are internal in the target. The cubic stars and other external clauses coincide. The same argument is insensitive to a common variable permutation, sign change, or clause permutation.

In the padded variant, splitting a binary implication into a directed path through a private literal preserves reachability between its two endpoints. Together with the opposite implication of a wire equality it makes the path's new literals equivalent, with the correct signs, to its endpoints. A split cubic-star axiom contributes one still-external ternary using its helper and one binary implication involving the third port; these belong to the **unchanged** star and do not connect different wire paths by two-way implications. More explicitly the helper is private to that star clause and occurs in just one binary implication, so neither sign of this helper creates a new directed cycle. Thus as in the unpadded case there is no change in the equivalences between external variables, even when some internal binary paths grow longer. The sufficient criterion applies for all k and all tag words. This proof does not require other parts of the source CNF to be satisfiable.

Instead of precomputing 2^d maps, beginning with one representative and splitting it produces at most two per tag. One directed comparison per tag suffices in this family. At fixed depth zero the map and every required clause proof are found in polynomial work per comparison (the [current finder](../src/pnp_lab/derived_transport.py) scans explicitly and reports propagation nodes, unit steps and clause scans). This is a bound for the tag-prefix phase, not a statement about every restriction of every input.

## Measured controls and budgets

The [report](../results/branch_interface.json) contains 184 independently checked ordered comparisons on the old odd-charge families, all ordered pairs through k=3 plus selected pairs through k=16. At k=8 the earlier padded family would have 256 incomparable raw branches at the tag cut under the direct substitution rule. The current streaming driver kept width 1 for all eight tags using eight checked moves; at the next bit the comparison budget was exhausted, width became 2 and the run ended UNKNOWN at cap 1. No claim of 256 actually materialized branches or a full SAT decision follows. On the satisfiable even-charge sibling the report records actual source words that fail the target before transport, and checks their transported words against the raw target afterward. These controls distinguish constructive witness transfer from a map that is sound only because a branch is empty.
